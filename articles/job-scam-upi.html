HIGH RISK

The Fake Job Offer UPI Scam

📖 4 min read 🛡 SatarkScan Awareness
⚠ The message that costs you money
📣 WFH Jobs India — Telegram
👋 Hi! We have a data entry opening — ₹15,000/month, work from home. Interested?
No experience needed. Just need a smartphone and 3 hours/day.
One-time registration fee to secure your slot:
Pay via UPI to register
₹500
🚫 This contact is no longer available

The real scenario

Aditya, a second-year B.Com student in Jaipur, got a Telegram message about a data entry job offering ₹15,000 a month with no experience required. The "recruiter" had a professional profile photo and had already sent him a PDF with the company's logo. After a short chat interview, Aditya was told he had been selected — but needed to pay ₹500 as a refundable "registration fee" to receive his work portal login. He scanned the QR code and paid. The recruiter said login credentials would arrive in 24 hours. Two days later, the Telegram account no longer existed. ₹500 gone, no job, no refund.

How do they build the illusion?

This scam targets students, homemakers, and recent graduates — anyone who is actively searching for work and is motivated enough to take action quickly. Fraudsters post in large WhatsApp groups, Telegram channels, and Facebook job boards with listings that are deliberately vague but irresistible: "₹800/day, just 2 hours work, no qualification needed."

The scammer's process is designed to feel legitimate. They send a formal-looking offer letter with a stolen company logo. They conduct a brief "interview" over Telegram or WhatsApp voice call. They create urgency — "only 3 slots left in your city." The "registration fee" is framed as refundable, or as payment for a "work kit," "ID card processing," or "background check." All of these are fabrications.

The amounts are deliberately small — ₹299, ₹500, ₹999 — because victims are less likely to involve police over small sums and more likely to dismiss it as a lesson learned. But each scammer runs this with hundreds of victims simultaneously, making lakhs per day with no overhead.

🚩 Red flags to watch for

🚩
Any job that requires an upfront payment — registration fee, security deposit, ID processing, work kit — before you receive a single rupee of salary.
🚩
The recruiter contacts you on Telegram, WhatsApp, or Instagram without you having applied anywhere. Legitimate HR teams use email or official portals like Naukri, LinkedIn, or Indeed.
🚩
The job description is deliberately vague: "online work," "data entry," "social media tasks" — no company name, no clear role, no employment contract offered upfront.
🚩
Pressure to pay immediately — "offer expires today," "only 2 slots left," "your selection will be cancelled if you don't confirm within 1 hour."

✅ How to stay safe

✅
The golden rule: Legitimate employers pay you. They never ask you to pay them. No real job — anywhere, ever — requires an upfront fee before you start working.
✅
Verify the company: Google the company name + "jobs" + "fraud." Search the exact UPI ID the scammer gives you through SatarkScan. Look up the company on the MCA portal (mca.gov.in) to confirm it's registered.
✅
Ask for an official email: Request that all communication happen over a company email address (not Gmail, Yahoo, or Hotmail). Scammers cannot produce this.
✅
Report it: File a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in or call the National Cyber Crime helpline 1930. Even ₹500 frauds are worth reporting — your report contributes to catching operations that are defrauding thousands of people simultaneously.
